## 🎓 Technical Details

### **Synthetic Data Strategy**
- **Borderline-SMOTE**: Generates samples on class boundary (~20% of minority)
- **Gaussian Augmentation**: Adds feature noise (~30% of full dataset)
- **Bootstrap**: Resampling with replacement (~20% of full dataset)
- **Result**: Preserves distributions, increases diversity, reduces overfitting

### **Calibration Methods**
- **Sigmoid (Platt)**: Linear sigmoid transform - simple, effective
- **Isotonic**: Non-parametric monotonic cubic spline - more flexible
- **Selection**: Automatic best-model selection on val set

### **Evaluation Metrics**
- **ROC-AUC**: Probability ranking quality
- **Average Precision**: Precision-recall curve
- **Brier Score**: MSE of probabilities (lower = better)
- **ECE**: Expected Calibration Error (reliability)
- **F1**: Class imbalance-resistant harmonic mean
